Major achievements

Papers with a center investigator as senior author.

Science Advances, Aug 2026 · TR&D 2 A spatially resolved implantable microdevice for multiplexed in situ screening of engineered cellular therapies in solid tumors Extends the microdevice platform from drugs to engineered cell therapies, screened side by side inside solid tumors. Senior author: Oliver Jonas · First author: Sajanlal R. Panikkanvalappil (BWH)
Magn Reson Med, Jul 2026 · TR&D 1 Optimization of diffusion MRI with consideration of the signal decay in biological tissues A principled way to choose diffusion MRI settings for real tissue, with a long-standing Swedish partner. Senior author: Stephan E. Maier · First author: Stefan Kuczera (University of Gothenburg)
bioRxiv, Jun 2026 · TR&D 2 An in vivo platform to jointly monitor cellular and metabolic responses to chemotherapy (preprint) Combines microdevice drug testing with metabolic readouts in the same tumor. Senior author: Oliver Jonas · First author: Veronika Pister (MIT)
IEEE Trans Med Robot Bionics, May 2026 · TR&D 3 Multimodal cross-attention fusion of B-mode ultrasound and strain elastography for tumor segmentation in robotics-assisted surgery Combines two ultrasound views with deep learning to outline tumors during robotic surgery. Senior author: Jagadeesan Jayender · First author: Sara Abkhofte (Western University, Canada)
Int J Comput Assist Radiol Surg, May 2026 · Robotic bronchoscopy Clinical feasibility study of generative depth estimation to diagnose airway stenosis using 3D Slicer A public 3D Slicer tool that estimates airway narrowing from bronchoscopy video, tested on 30 patients; with Canon Medical Research USA and EPFL. Senior author: Nobuhiko Hata · First author: Artur Banach (BWH)
Ann Biomed Eng, Mar 2026 · TR&D 2 Development and pre-clinical feasibility of a catheter-based platform for interventional phase-0 drug response measurements Brings in situ drug testing to catheter-based interventional radiology procedures. Senior author: Oliver Jonas · First author: Sharath K. Bhagavatula (BWH)
J Med Robot Res, Mar 2026 · TR&D 3 Clinical validation of a novel robotic device for MR-guided prostate biopsy: initial patient experience First patient use of a robotic needle-guide template, developed with Physical Sciences Inc. Senior author: Junichi Tokuda · First author: Pedro Moreira (BWH)
Phys Med Biol, Mar 2026 · TR&D 3 AI-assisted cryo-dose estimation and tissue injury modeling for assessing side effects in MRI-guided prostate cryoablation Links predicted freezing dose to side effects, toward safer prostate cryoablation. Senior author: Junichi Tokuda · First author: Pedro Moreira (BWH)
IEEE Trans Pattern Anal Mach Intell, Feb 2026 · Image computing Unified cross-modal medical image synthesis with hierarchical mixture of product-of-experts One model that synthesizes missing imaging modalities, such as MRI and ultrasound, for surgical guidance. Senior author: William M. Wells III · First author: Reuben Dorent
Urol Oncol, Jan 2026 · Clinical research Transperineal 3T MRI-guided and transrectal MRI-ultrasound fusion prostate biopsies: do lesion location and size impact diagnostic yield? Shows which prostate lesions benefit most from in-bore MRI-guided biopsy. Senior author: Clare M. Tempany · First author: Quinn Rainer (BWH)
Int J Comput Assist Radiol Surg, Dec 2025 · Global health Benchmarking NousNav: quantifying the spatial accuracy and clinical performance of an affordable, open-source neuronavigation system Validates a low-cost, open-source navigation system for brain surgery, built with Kitware and Isomics. Senior author: Alexandra J. Golby · First author: Colton Barr (Queen's University, Canada)
