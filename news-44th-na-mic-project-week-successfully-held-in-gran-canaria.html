44th NA-MIC Project Week Successfully Held in Gran Canaria

March 2, 2026News

We are pleased to announce the successful completion of the 44th NA-MIC Project Week (PW44), held January 26–30, 2026, in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain.

This fully in-person event brought together the international 3D Slicer and NA-MIC community for an intensive week of collaboration, innovation, and hands-on development across medical imaging, image-guided therapy, AI, robotics, visualization, and computational modeling.

https://projectweek.na-mic.org/PW44_2026_GranCanaria/
