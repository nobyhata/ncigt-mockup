Research

Image Guided Therapy (IGT) is a foundational discipline that involves the integration of imaging into procedural medicine used in surgery, radiation oncology, and interventional radiology. The notion that IGT is more efficient, more effective, and less expensive than conventional surgery has been confirmed both in extensive long-term studies and in ongoing revolutionary applications in the operating room. We have laid critical groundwork with this extraordinary technology during the last two decades.

2015-2020

During this period our work was divided into four technology research and development (TRD) projects. The Prostate TRD developed novel platforms to allow for molecular profiling of prostate cancer and imaging to allow for improved predictive markers for guiding biopsy and focal therapy, and by use of image processing methods for monitoring treatment effect. The Neurosurgery TRD developed mass spectrometry as a biomarker for intraoperative surgical decision making to better define tumor extent. Advanced functional mapping using fMRI+DTI was used for individualized treatment planning. The Computation TRD developed specialized multimodal image registration algorithms and custom hardware for motion compensation to improve tumor localization and functional mapping. The Guidance TRD developed novel instrument and tumor tracking technologies to accurately deliver therapy despite significant physiological motion and tissue deformation.

2020-2025

Under the restructured Advanced Technologies–National Center for Image-Guided Therapy (AT-NCIGT), this cycle delivered quantifiable improvements in surgical and patient outcomes through detailed assessment of tissue architecture heterogeneity, deep-learning-based image registration, uniform data curation and annotation, and software and devices for intraprocedural guidance. Research was organized into three TRD projects. TRD 1, Imaging Cancer Heterogeneity, characterized tumors and their surroundings at macroscopic, microscopic, and molecular scales. TRD 2, Deep Learning, developed weakly-supervised deep learning methods for image-guided brain tumor surgery, lung surgery, and transperineal in-bore prostate biopsy, along with infrastructure for curating imaging data. TRD 3, Intraoperative Devices, delivered devices via 3D Slicer and OpenIGTLink, including intraoperative needle guidance for prostate biopsy, navigation for thoracoscopic lung surgery, and implantable microdevices for difficult-to-access tissues such as the brain.

2026-2030

Building on decades of technical and clinical leadership, the Center now enters its next cycle as the Advanced Technologies–National Center for Image-Guided Therapy (AT-NCIGT), continuing to lead advancements in image-guided approaches including MR-guided neurosurgery, prostate interventions, and focused ultrasound therapy. This renewal of our NIH P41 Center targets three critical needs in IGT and cancer therapy: high-fidelity MR imaging, in vivo deployment of image-guided microdevices (IMDs), and the development of enabling technologies, leveraging high-field MRI platforms alongside novel IMD systems and advanced enabling tools. For the period 2026-2030, our research is divided into three technology research and development (TRD) projects.

TRD 1 - Imaging Cancer Heterogeneity with High-Fidelity MRI

Imaging Cancer Heterogeneity with High-Fidelity MRI focuses on advanced quantitative MRI techniques — diffusion and chemical exchange saturation transfer (CEST) imaging — to support diagnosis, preoperative imaging, intervention guidance, and treatment monitoring, enabling robust, high-resolution, and reproducible imaging biomarkers.

LEARN MORE

TRD 2 - Multiscale Tumor Analysis

Multiscale Tumor Analysis for Personalized Therapy extends implantable microdevice (IMD) technology into a standardized platform for in situ drug response testing, pairing image-guided delivery and retrieval with a multi-omic tissue analysis pipeline to guide personalized treatment selection and predict drug resistance.

LEARN MORE

TRD 3 - Data-Driven Image-Guided Therapy

Enabling Technologies for Data-Driven Image-Guided Therapy develops data-driven, closed-loop systems for precision needle placement, augmented-reality-guided navigation for lymph node resection, and open software infrastructure — built on 3D Slicer and OpenIGTLink — to support dynamic, patient-specific image guidance.
