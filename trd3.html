TR&D 3 · Technology research and development

Enabling technologies for data-driven interventions

Robotic needle guidance and augmented-reality navigation that help physicians reach small targets precisely, across MRI, CT, ultrasound and endoscopy.

Meet the team Work with this project
[Key figure from a robotic or navigated procedure]

The clinical problem

Many procedures depend on placing a needle or instrument exactly on a small target that moves or deforms, while the physician watches images that are not perfectly aligned with the patient. Needles bend, organs shift and anatomy differs from the preoperative scan. Robots that correct their path from imaging data, and navigation that tracks the anatomy as it moves, can make these procedures more accurate and more consistent.

What we are developing

  • Robotic needle guidance with path correction for needle deflection
  • Augmented-reality and trackerless navigation for open and endoscopic surgery
  • AI-based prediction to plan and monitor ablation
  • Clinical evaluation in the AMIGO suite and procedure rooms
