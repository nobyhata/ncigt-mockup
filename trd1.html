TR&D 1 · Technology research and development

Imaging cancer heterogeneity

Advanced MRI at 3T and 7T that reveals the structure and chemistry of tumors in more detail, to guide where and how to treat.

Meet the team Work with this project
[Key figure from a diffusion or CEST MRI study]

The clinical problem

Tumors are not uniform. Regions that look alike on standard MRI can differ in cell density, microstructure and metabolism, and those differences affect diagnosis, targeting and response to treatment. Measuring them noninvasively, with methods that are reproducible across scanners, would let clinicians aim biopsies and therapy at the parts of a tumor that matter most.

What we are developing

  • Sub-millimeter diffusion MRI for tissue microstructure
  • Quantitative chemical exchange saturation transfer (CEST) imaging
  • Methods for 3T clinical scanners, 7T and high-performance gradient systems
  • Reproducible quantitative measures for prostate and brain tumors
