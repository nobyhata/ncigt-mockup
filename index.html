Research

Three technology research and development projects

Three projects, each taking new technology from the lab to patients.

TR&D 1

Imaging cancer heterogeneity

Sub-millimeter diffusion MRI and quantitative CEST at 3T and 7T to map tumor structure and chemistry.

Explore the project and its latest paper →

Image: Latest TR&D 1 paper: radial diffusivity and fractional anisotropy maps from a high-performance gradient 3T scanner. Zhu et al., Hum Brain Mapp, Nov 2026, Fig. 2 (cropped), CC BY 4.0. Paper

TR&D 2

Implantable microdevices

Microdevices that test many drugs inside a patient’s tumor, with machine learning that links response to imaging.

Explore the project and its latest paper →

Image: Latest TR&D 2 paper: an implantable microdevice releasing labeled cells. Panikkanvalappil et al., Sci Adv, Aug 2026, Fig. 2 (cropped), CC BY-NC 4.0. Paper

TR&D 3

Enabling technologies for data-driven interventions

Robotic needle guidance and augmented-reality navigation for precise procedures across imaging modalities.

Explore the project and its latest paper →

Image: Recent TR&D 3 paper: predicted needle deflection with confidence bands on segmented prostate MRI. Hoffman et al., Med Phys, Feb 2026, Fig. 7 (cropped), CC BY 4.0. Paper

Resources and equipment

Equipment and tools available to collaborators

Each resource lists the investigator to contact.

High-performance MRI

Ultra-high-performance gradient (MAGNUS) and 7T MRI for advanced diffusion and quantitative imaging.

Contact: Stephan Maier

MRI-guided robotics

MRI-compatible needle-guidance robots and shape-sensing needles for prostate and pelvic procedures.

Contact: Junichi Tokuda

Robotic navigation and bronchoscopy

Continuum robots and vision-based navigation for bronchoscopy and endoluminal procedures.

Contact: Nobuhiko Hata

Implantable microdevices

Microdevice design, implantation and multi-omic analysis of in situ drug response.

Contact: Oliver Jonas

Surgical vision and augmented reality

Stereo endoscopy, 3D surface reconstruction and AR navigation for surgery.

Contact: Jagadeesan Jayender
