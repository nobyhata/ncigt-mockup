TR&D 2 · Technology research and development

Implantable microdevices

Tiny devices placed in a tumor that test many drugs at once inside the patient, so treatment can be chosen by how that tumor actually responds.

Meet the team Work with this project
[Key figure from a microdevice implantation study]

The clinical problem

Patients with the same cancer type often respond very differently to the same drug, and today most treatment choices are made without testing them on the patient’s own tumor. Microdevices that release minute doses of many drugs into different regions of a tumor, then are removed with the surrounding tissue, can show which drugs work before a full course of systemic therapy begins.

What we are developing

  • Microdevices that measure drug efficacy in situ
  • Image-guided implantation and retrieval, including MRI-guided placement
  • Multi-omic readout of local drug response
  • Machine learning that links response patterns to imaging biomarkers
